⚡ HARDWARE TESTPOINT TRIGGER PROCEDURE

STEP 1: POWER OFF Disconnect battery connector flex. Wait 5 seconds for capacitors to discharge.
STEP 2: BRIDGE TESTPOINT Using precision curved tweezers, bridge Pin #1 (EDL) to nearest gold ground GND pad.
STEP 3: INSERT USB CABLE Plug high-speed Type-C cable connected to PC while maintaining tweezer bridge.
STEP 4: RELEASE TWEEZERS Once Windows chimes and device populates COM port (EDL 9008 / BROM), release tweezers.
